## Where it fits

Use this when you need a promotional item the recipient will not use once and throw
away. The case stays and gets reused; when the capsules run out, only the refill
needs topping up.

- ESG campaign kits for employees and partner companies
- Keepsakes for formal occasions such as shareholder meetings and anniversaries
- Limited-run brand collaboration goods

## What is in it

Four capsules sit inside a hard case. A logo and campaign wording can be printed on
the case exterior, and on large orders the capsule colour can be matched to your
corporate CI.

## Before you proceed

- Minimum order is 1,000 units
- Logo printing requires print-ready artwork (AI or PDF)
- Whether custom capsule colour is possible depends on the order volume
