## Where it fits

This replaces single-use amenities. Instead of refilling small bottles in every
room, one unit goes on the wall and you top it up with capsules.

- Hotel and resort guest bathrooms
- Shared showers in lodging facilities
- Gym and pool shower rooms

## How it works

The disc is divided into four bays, each holding a different capsule. Turn the
disc to bring the bay you want to the outlet, press the button, and **exactly one**
capsule drops out.

- Catch it in your hand, hold it under water, and it dissolves in 3-5 seconds
- Only what is pressed comes out, so no cleanser is left over to be thrown away
- The next person always gets a fresh capsule

## What changed from the 3-bay vertical unit

The earlier wall-mounted 3-bay unit had vertical tubes and took up a lot of width.
The circular 4-bay fits one more bay in the same footprint, and it can also sit on
a counter rather than being fixed to a wall.

## What this changes

- **Single-use bottles disappear.** Waste and disposal cost drop by the number of bottles per room
- **Only a measured dose comes out.** Unlike a pump, one press is one capsule, so nothing is left to discard
- **Storage gets lighter.** No space is needed for bulk drums
- **Every use starts fresh.** There is no shared nozzle being pressed over and over

## How a rollout runs

1. We check the room count and bathroom layout
2. We install in one room or one floor first and confirm how it is used
3. Based on that, we extend to the rest of the rooms

Installation and staff training are included.
