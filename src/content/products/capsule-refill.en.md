## What is different

There is no plastic bottle holding the cleanser. The film is the packaging, and it
dissolves away on contact with water. Nothing is left to throw out.

Only one capsule is used at a time, so no more comes out than is needed, and
several people are not handling the same container over and over.

## Types

| Type | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Body wash | Shower |
| Shampoo | Scalp and hair |
| Face and hand soap | Hands and face |

Each type is colour-coded, so they stay easy to tell apart once loaded into a dispenser.

## Where it is used

This is the consumable shared by the case products — the box and slide kit — and by
the dispenser. Once a dispenser is installed, these capsules are all you top up.
