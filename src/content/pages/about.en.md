## What we make

VALORA makes **cleanser capsules wrapped in water-soluble film**, and the dispensers
built to use them.

It started from a simple observation: holding cleanser does not require manufacturing
a new plastic bottle every time. The film is the packaging, and it dissolves within
3-5 seconds on contact with water.

## Three problems, one answer

| | Conventional | VALORA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waste | Single-use bottles / bulk drums | No container |
| Hygiene | Many people reuse the same container | One capsule per use |
| Dosage | Pumps are hard to control, so cleanser is overused | A measured dose |

Until now, cutting single-use amenities meant worse hygiene, and protecting hygiene
meant more waste. The capsule does not force that trade-off.

## Technology

- **Patent filed** — Capsule-type soap and its dispensing device (application no. 10-2025-0122876)
- **Industry-academia technology transfer** — Composition containing plant-extract active ingredients, transferred from Nambu University
- **Certification in progress** — Currently going through certification under the Cosmetics Act

## How much it reduces

Estimates based on the introduction of 10,000 capsules.

| Item | Reduction | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plastic waste | About 80 kg | Replacing small amenity bottles and pouches |
| CO₂ emissions | 216-280 kg | Applying 2.7-3.5 kg CO₂e per kg of plastic |
| Cleanser overuse | 20-30 % | Reduced overuse from a single measured dose |

These map to UN Sustainable Development Goals 12 (responsible consumption and
production) and 13 (climate action), so they can be reported as figures in an ESG
report.

> The figures above are estimates proportional to the quantity introduced. If you need
> the underlying calculation, please ask when you contact us.

## How supply works

| Step | What happens | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Consultation | Confirm use case and volume, propose a configuration | — |
| 2. Sampling | Produce samples and confirm usability | About 2 weeks |
| 3. Order | Confirm quantity and delivery date | — |
| 4. Production | Manufacturing and quality inspection | About 4-6 weeks |
| 5. Delivery | On-site installation and user training (dispensers) | — |
| 6. Refills | Regular capsule supply | — |

For dispensers, delivery is not the end. We install the unit and train the people who
will use it, so it does not end up sitting unused on site.
