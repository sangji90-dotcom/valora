## 1. Personal data collected

- Required: name (company name), email address, enquiry content
- Optional: phone number

## 2. Purpose of collection and use

Used solely to reply to enquiries and to keep a record of consultations.

## 3. Retention period

Retained for one year after the enquiry is resolved, then destroyed without delay.
If the data subject requests deletion, the data is destroyed immediately.

## 4. Provision to third parties

The company does not provide personal data to third parties without the data
subject's consent.
