> The Korean version of this policy is the governing text. This English page is provided
> for convenience only; where the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

## 1. Personal data collected

- Required: name (company name), email address, enquiry content
- Optional: phone number

Nothing is collected outside the enquiry form.

## 2. Purpose of collection and use

Used solely to reply to enquiries and to keep a record of consultations.

## 3. Retention period

Retained for one year after the enquiry is resolved, then destroyed without delay.
If the data subject requests deletion, the data is destroyed immediately.

## 4. How data is destroyed

Personal data is destroyed without delay once the retention period has passed or the
purpose of processing has been achieved. Electronic files are deleted by a method that
makes recovery impossible; any printed copies are shredded.

## 5. Provision to third parties

The company does not provide personal data to third parties without the data
subject's consent.

## 6. Outsourced processing

Enquiry intake and storage are outsourced to the following processor.

| Processor | Outsourced work |
| --- | --- |
| NAVER Corp. | Operating the enquiry form and storing the responses |

The processor's servers are located in the Republic of Korea. No personal data
collected through this site is transferred abroad.

## 7. Automatically collected data

This site does not use cookies, access-log analytics or any other device that
collects visitor activity automatically.

## 8. Measures to secure personal data

- Access to personal data is limited to the smallest possible number of staff
- Enquiry responses are protected by an account password and two-factor authentication
- The site is served over end-to-end encryption (HTTPS)

## 9. Rights of the data subject

The data subject may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or
suspension of the processing of their personal data. Requests sent to the contact
below are acted on without delay. Requests for access are received at the same address.

## 10. Data protection officer

- Name: Jeon So-eun (Chief Executive Officer)
- Contact: ceo@valora.kr

A data subject seeking redress for harm caused by the handling of personal data may
apply to the following bodies for dispute resolution or advice. These are Korean
authorities and operate in Korean.
